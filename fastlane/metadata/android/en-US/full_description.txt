NetView gives you a real-time, per-SIM view of every serving-cell parameter your Android device exposes — no root, no hidden menus, no account.

CELLULAR (per SIM)
• Network generation — 2G / 3G / 4G LTE / 5G NSA / 5G SA
• Serving cell — PCI, EARFCN / NR-ARFCN, eNB ID, Sector ID, TAC, Band number
• Signal — RSRP, RSRQ, SINR, RSSI, CQI, Timing Advance
• Carrier Aggregation — PCell + up to 4 SCells with per-CC band, bandwidth, PCI, and frequency (Android 12+)
• Voice technology — VoLTE / VoNR / CS fallback, IMS registration state

WI-FI
• SSID, BSSID, channel, frequency, security type
• Link speed, RSSI, and latency ping to the gateway

LOCATION
• GPS latitude, longitude, altitude, and accuracy

MULTI-SIM
• Dedicated tab for each SIM slot — works correctly on DSDS devices

DESIGN
• Material You — adaptive colours from your wallpaper
• Configurable auto-refresh: 1 – 60 seconds
• Share snapshot as plain text for easy bug reports
• No ads, no tracking, no network calls

PERMISSIONS USED
• READ_PHONE_STATE / READ_PRECISE_PHONE_STATE — read cell parameters
• ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION — required by Android to expose cell IDs
• ACCESS_WIFI_STATE — read Wi-Fi details
• INTERNET — latency ping to the Wi-Fi gateway only; no external servers contacted

REQUIREMENTS
• Android 8.0 (API 26) or later
• A SIM card inserted (Wi-Fi tab is always visible even without a SIM)
• Location permission granted at runtime

NOTE: Carrier Aggregation details require Android 12+. SIB parameters (RS Power, P-Max, etc.) are not accessible via public Android APIs and are therefore not shown.
